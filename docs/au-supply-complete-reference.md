# A-U.Supply / Complete — Comprehensive Reference Document

> **Purpose**: This document is a detailed reference for LLMs and future prompts. It consolidates known information about the A-U.Supply label, the band Complete, and associated projects, tools, and aesthetic frameworks. It does **not** document the website's technical architecture — that lives in the codebase and project memory.

### 1.2 Philosophy & Aesthetic

A-U.Supply is not a traditional record label. It functions as a **label, web platform, tooling workshop, and collective art project** simultaneously. Key principles:

- **Anti-professionalism**: The website is deliberately chaotic and geocities-inspired, with draggable elements, ASCII art, broken navigation hierarchies, and pages titled things like "The background-color Property." There is no artist roster page, no press kit, no conventional marketing. The label name itself is a joke about industrial supply — as if you could order audio units by the pallet.
- **Database aesthetics (and beyond)**: Album and project titles often feel like they were generated by or extracted from existing databases, inventories, or technical systems. *Law Bale Straw Wonder*, *Tomato Sink Cloud Tag*, *Erkind NOS*, *How How Things are Made are Made* — these read like warehouse manifests, corrupted metadata fields, or search engine detritus. But the naming impulse extends beyond databases into the full language of systems: call numbers, model numbers, form fields, error messages, and bureaucratic artifacts. The titles occupy a space between found language and invented taxonomy.
- **Tubes**: Everything is a tube. The signal path, the internet, the body, the production pipeline, the modular synth rack, the supply chain. Audio goes into a tube and comes out different. Tubes are the fundamental unit of A-U.Supply's cosmology. This is not a metaphor. Or it is only a metaphor. The tube doesn't care.
- **Collage-as-method**: Both the music and the web presence treat collage — of sound, text, interface, and reference — as the primary compositional technique. Found audio, sampled instructional material, AI-generated text, and hand-written fragments coexist without hierarchy.
- **Political undercurrent**: The Song Title Library reveals a persistent engagement with anti-capitalist, Marxist, and absurdist political thought. Recurring motifs include: labor, exploitation, consumption, the "serf," class consciousness, and the language of financial systems repurposed as surrealist poetry. References include "Serf's Up!" (a pun on surf/serf), "world capitalist intercourse," chrematistics (Aristotle's term for the unnatural art of money-making, distorted here into "prematistics"), and "the expenditures of a primitive being" (a nod to Bataille's *The Accursed Share*).
- **Art criticism as irritant**: The contact page includes the line "art critism is 'harmful' because it disturbs wealthy people in their enjoyment of eye way way" — a distorted quote of mysterious origin that positions the project as deliberately uncomfortable, opposed to aesthetic consumption as leisure.
- **BEDO**: A recurring in-group reference — "we're all brendans after all." The phrase "people are always brendan / people are always human" appears in the song title library. Nobody knows what BEDO is. Nobody.

### 1.5 Tools & Software (GitHub Repositories)

A-U.Supply maintains a GitHub organization with purpose-built creative tools:

#### glottisdale
- **Language**: Python (library) with Rust audio internals. GPL-3.0.
- **Purpose**: Syllable-level audio collage engine. Pulls video from Slack, segments speech into syllables using audio analysis, reassembles them into collages. Also includes a vocal MIDI mapping engine ("sing" subcommand). The name combines "glottis" (the vocal apparatus) with "-dale" (a place suffix).
- **Usage**: Installed as a pip package. Bot wrappers in the website repo (`glottisdale-bot/`, `hymnal-bot/`) handle Slack I/O.

#### BadTV
- **Language**: Rust (MIT)
- **Purpose**: Stitch TV news clips into infomercial-style audio collages. Takes a phrase → searches the Internet Archive's TV News Archive (GDELT TV API) for each word → extracts exact audio using Whisper-based word-level alignment → applies infomercial-style audio processing (pitch normalization, compression, EQ, reverb) → outputs a stitched WAV file.
- **Significance**: Instrumentalizes the TV News Archive as a sample bank, turning broadcast language into raw material for sound collage.

#### youboob
- **Language**: Rust
- **Purpose**: YouTube audio stem splitter using Meta's HTDemucs model. Extracts audio from YouTube videos and separates into stems (vocals, drums, bass, other). Another tube in the sample-and-collage pipeline.

#### Website Bots (in ausupply.github.io)
- **midi-bot**: Daily MIDI generator — scrapes headlines, LLM generates musical parameters, Magenta.js neural networks generate 4 tracks (melody, drums, bass, chords), posts to Slack #midieval.
- **glottisdale-bot**: Thin wrapper around the glottisdale library — fetches videos from Slack, runs collage pipeline, posts results.
- **hymnal-bot**: Thin wrapper — fetches MIDI, extends via Magenta.js, runs the glottisdale vocal mapping ("sing") pipeline, posts results.
- **muzzik-bot**: Scrapes YouTube links from Slack #muzzik, maintains an unlisted YouTube playlist.
- **song-titles-bot**: Scrapes song titles from Slack #song-titles, filters via HF Inference API, generates the interactive Song Title Library page.
- **surreal-prompt-bot**: Scrapes news headlines, mixes with artistic inspirations, uses Groq's Llama API to generate surrealist drawing prompts for Slack #drawma.

All bots run daily via GitHub Actions. The Slack workspace is the collective's primary creative ecosystem — bots generate material continuously, feeding it back into the group's practice.

### 2.3 Sound & Method

Complete's music is built on **collage, found audio, tubes, industrial texture, spoken word, and lo-fi production**. Key characteristics:

- **Found audio as primary material**: Instructional recordings, engineering lectures, OSHA-style training audio, broadcast fragments, and field recordings are treated as equal compositional elements alongside synthesized sound and vocal performance.
- **Tubes**: Signal goes in one end and comes out the other, changed. The modular synth is a system of tubes. The production pipeline is a tube. The internet is a series of tubes. Audio is fed through tubes of processing — resampling, degradation, filtering, collaging — until it becomes something else. The tube is both method and metaphor, and the group does not distinguish between the two.
- **Lo-fi production as aesthetic choice**: Deliberately degraded audio quality, tape-like artifacts, and murky mixing are consistent across releases. The lo-fi quality is not incidental but central to the project's refusal of polish and professionalism.
- **Semantic satiation / repetition**: Words and phrases are repeated until they lose meaning (e.g., "Heat" in the opening of *Law Bale Straw Wonder*), a technique that collapses language into pure sound material.
- **Non-hierarchical sequencing**: Confessional vulnerability, ambient drone, sampled lectures, and sitcom parody are all treated at the same level. There is no hierarchy of moment.
- **Progressive degradation**: Bouncing, resampling, and degrading audio over weeks or months. Time and repetition are compositional tools. The signal path is a one-way tube toward entropy.
- **Genre-resistant**: Tagged as "soundtrack" on Bandcamp. The music resists genre categorization but operates in the territory of industrial, noise, ambient, musique concrète, outsider Americana, and experimental electronics.

### 2.4 Thematic Concerns

- **Labor and the working body**: Physical strain, heat, leverage, load-bearing — the language of industrial work recurs throughout.
- **Depersonalization and psychological fracture**: The *A Deper[ ]alized Ratio* title, the "left is right and right is left" confusion of *Sikabidi*, confessional moments about autism and feeling unliked.
- **Consumption and capitalism**: Chrematistics distorted into "prematistics" (the fictional science of financial gain), soda pricing in "Yeehaw Systems," the Bataille-influenced "expenditures of a primitive being."
- **Americana as ruin**: Country-music tropes (outlaws, trucks), bison-watching, semi-trucks, Houston blackouts — American iconography presented as degraded, failing, or surreal. #transcendentalhonkytonk.
- **The sitcom as political form**: The "Be nice to me 2 (sitcom theme)" segment and its critique of political discourse as entertainment format.
- **Existential resignation**: "In the long run we're all dead" (Keynes), the persistent cigarettes-at-the-end-of-the-world imagery.

## 3. Distribution & Infrastructure

- **Music distribution**: Bandcamp (recent releases, 24-bit/44.1kHz), archive.org (2020 releases), SoundCloud (sketches/sessions), YouTube (singles, double album, compilation).
- **File formats**: Bandcamp releases available in FLAC, MP3, etc. Archive.org releases as VBR MP3 ZIPs.
- **Slack workspace**: The collective's primary creative ecosystem. Multiple channels (#midieval, #muzzik, #drawma, #song-titles, #glottisdale) are serviced by daily bots that scrape, generate, and post creative material continuously.
- **Custom tooling**: All major tools written in Rust (BadTV, youboob, glottisdale core) or Python (bot wrappers, scrapers). Standalone binaries and pip-installable packages — no dependency on external frameworks.
- **AI integration**: AI is treated as a collaborator and generator, not a replacement for human work. Aico.bot wrote liner notes for the 2020 releases. The midi-bot uses neural networks (Magenta.js) to generate MIDI. The surreal-prompt-bot uses Llama for drawing prompts. The song-titles-bot uses HF Inference for filtering. AI-generated material enters the same collage pipeline as everything else — another tube.
- **Internet Archive**: Both distribution platform and sample library. The 2020 catalog lives on archive.org. BadTV queries the TV News Archive. The alignment with IA's mission of universal access is infrastructural, not just philosophical.

## 4. Aesthetic & Conceptual Framework

### 4.1 Terminology

- **Chrematistics / Prematistics**: Aristotle distinguished *oikonomia* (household management, natural) from *chrematistics* (wealth-acquisition for its own sake, unnatural and limitless). A-U.Supply distorts this into "prematistics" — a fictional science of financial gain, introduced in "Auri Sacra Fames" (disc 2 of the double album). The language of wealth rendered as pseudoscience; the tube of capital.
- **Semantical truzzles**: From the website tagline — a portmanteau or misspelling that positions the project as navigating semantic confusion on the listener's behalf.
- **Audex**: The label's term for its yearly catalog/release pages (audex.html, audex21.html, etc.). A play on "index" or "audio index" — the catalog as database.
- **The robot ranch**: NoNameSteak's domain — the modular synth rig, the machines, the AI collaborators, the whole menagerie of signal-generating apparatus that feeds material into the production tubes.

### 4.2 Recurring Visual Motifs

- **The cheeseburger**: The header image across all pages is "cheeze-bourger2.png" — a deliberately misspelled, lo-fi cheeseburger image that functions as the label's de facto logo.
- **ASCII art**: Geometric shapes, boxes, and text-art diagrams.
- **Animated GIFs**: Early-web-era animations ("dust_bobbles2.gif" and similar).
- **System interfaces**: Terminal UIs, PF-key menus, visitor counters, geocities aesthetics, deliberately broken/ugly web design.

## 5. How to Use This Document

This document provides context for LLM-assisted creative work involving A-U.Supply and Complete. When generating content — reviews, liner notes, bios, fictional interviews, song titles, website copy, catalog descriptions, or any other material — the following principles should guide the output:

### Tone & Voice
- **Deadpan and absurdist**. The humor is dry and arrives without announcement. Nothing is explained. The joke and the serious statement are indistinguishable and the project does not care if you can tell them apart.
- **Politically engaged but never preachy**. Marxist analysis is embedded in surreal imagery — heat, labor, consumption, trucks, bison, cigarettes, tubes — rather than stated directly. The politics are in the material, not in the commentary.
- **Anti-professional**. Nothing should sound like a press release, a pitch deck, or a branding exercise. If it sounds like marketing, put it through the tube again.

### Language
- **Technical/industrial vocabulary mixed with pastoral/Americana imagery**. Engineering manuals, warehouse inventories, and OSHA training materials share space with country-music tropes, prairie landscapes, and livestock.
- **Neologisms welcome**. Invent words that sound like they already exist in some bureaucratic or technical context. They should feel extracted from a system, not coined by a poet.
- **Titles should feel like database entries** — warehouse manifests, corrupted metadata fields, Library of Congress call numbers, model numbers, form field labels. But not exclusively: they can also feel like misheard song lyrics, bad translations, or the output of a malfunctioning search engine.

### Structure
- **Non-hierarchical**. Vulnerable confessions sit next to engineering lectures sit next to sitcom parodies. No single register dominates. The confessional and the technical have equal weight.
- **Collage logic**. Juxtaposition over transition. Don't smooth the edges between tonal shifts — the seams are the point.

### Production References
- **Lo-fi, degraded, process-based**. Progressive resampling, found audio collage, industrial textures, drone, spoken word over ambient beds. Everything has been through the tubes.
- **The tube as universal metaphor**. Signal enters the tube and exits changed. The production pipeline, the internet, the vocal tract, the modular synth — all tubes. When in doubt, refer to tubes.

### Political Framework
- **Marxist, Bataillean, anti-capitalist** — but expressed through absurdism and material imagery rather than explicit polemic. Chrematistics, not sloganeering. The expenditures of a primitive being, not a manifesto.
- **Labor is always present**. The language of work — strain, leverage, load-bearing, shift schedules, production lines — recurs as both subject matter and compositional metaphor.

### What NOT to Do
- Do not sound polished or professional. Do not explain jokes. Do not provide artist bios that read like Spotify "About" sections.
- Do not name-drop influences or make genre comparisons. A-U.Supply exists in its own supply chain. The tubes lead nowhere familiar.
- Do not use the word "curated."
- Do not resolve ambiguity. If something could mean two things, let it mean both. If the members' identities are unclear, leave them unclear. The project's relationship to its own mythology is deliberately unstable.
- Do not assume you know how many members Complete has.
